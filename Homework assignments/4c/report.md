While looking into how artists react to and view AI in art, 
I found an interesting [article](https://innovatingwithai.com/artists-view-of-ai-generated-pieces/) on the subject.
In this article, they argued that for a lot of artists that they interviewed and responded to their surveys, many 
of them actually use AI as a tool in their creative process. This made sense to me as it is a tool and something 
that I think can and should be used. For me, I think that it is a great tool but it can never 
really live up to what real art really is. Real art is something that is indescribable and cannot be 
replicated by a computer. I think this article sums it up great at the end, it says "Theatre survived the advent of film, film survived the advent of television, and television survived the advent of online video. So, too, 
will human creativity survive the rise of AI-generated art." There is something about seeing what 
someones brain came up with (not a machine) that is really satisfying and special. 

I think that when AI is used, it should be disclosed as to how you used it in the process of a product. 

As far as accountability goes, I think that it is important to have a way to track how AI is used. I found 
an [article] (https://aithenticate.org/artificial-intelligence/label-for-ai-enhancing-transparency-and-accountability-in-ai-generated-content/) 
that talks about tagging AI generated content with labels so that people can know that it was created with AI. 
I think that this is a good thing. It is important to know the source of things when you look at, study or use them.
