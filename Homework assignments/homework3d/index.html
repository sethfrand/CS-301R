Beginner path

Cookies & Bars

Outcome: learn fast visual feedback and ingredient ratio logic.

This path is designed for fast reps. You will see how butter temperature, sugar type, and mixing time change spread, chew, color, and structure.

Lesson sequence

  • Drop cookies: creaming, chill time, and spread control
  • Brownies: fudgy vs. cakey structure through egg and flour ratios
  • Bars: pan lining, layering, and clean slicing

Milestone bake

Build one base dough and split it three ways to compare crisp, chewy, and soft results.

Common fixes

Stop flat cookies, raw centers, and dry edges by changing pan heat, dough temp, and rest time.

Beginner path

Cakes & Frosting

Outcome: produce even layers with stable crumb and smooth finishing.

This path focuses on batter architecture. You will learn how air is built, protected, and baked into a cake that can actually support filling and frosting.

Lesson sequence

  • Vanilla cake: mixing methods and pan prep
  • Chocolate layer cake: balancing moisture with structure
  • Buttercream lab: texture, temperature, and spreading control

Milestone bake

Stack a two-layer celebration cake with straight sides, clean slices, and balanced sweetness.

Common fixes

Address tunneling, domed tops, curdled frosting, and gummy crumb with process adjustments.

Intermediate path

Bread Basics

Outcome: read fermentation and shape dough with confidence.

This path moves from ingredients to living dough behavior. The focus is on fermentation cues, handling, and oven spring rather than blindly following a timer.

Lesson sequence

  • Yeasted sandwich loaf: proofing fundamentals and pan support
  • Rustic boule: hydration, folds, and shaping tension
  • Focaccia: fermentation timing and high-hydration confidence

Milestone bake

Bake one dough in two forms and compare crumb, crust, and proofing response across shapes.

Common fixes

Correct dense crumb, blown-out scores, overproofing, and flour-heavy shaping habits.

Advanced path

Pastry Lab

Outcome: execute precision desserts where texture timing matters.

This path is about discipline under tighter tolerances. You will manage cold butter, cooked fillings, and staged assembly so refined pastries stay crisp, light, and clean.

Lesson sequence

  • Pate sucree: blind baking and clean shell structure
  • Cream fillings: curd, diplomat, and pastry cream control
  • Rough puff and lamination: turns, resting, and layer protection

Milestone bake

Finish a fruit tart with crisp shell, stable filling, clean glaze, and precise final assembly.

Common fixes

Prevent butter leakage, soggy bottoms, split custards, and collapsed layers.

Core skills

Master the techniques that transfer across every recipe.

01

Mise en place

Measure first, then bake. Setup reduces mistakes and helps you notice timing-sensitive steps before they matter.

02

Heat awareness

Ovens drift. Learn rack placement, hot spots, and why rotating pans matters more than blindly trusting the dial.

03

Texture cues

Baking improves faster when you read batter thickness, dough elasticity, and crumb structure instead of only watching the clock.

90-minute lesson flow

  1. 15 min Ingredient prep and tool setup
  2. 20 min Technique demo with side-by-side examples
  3. 35 min Guided bake with checkpoints
  4. 20 min Tasting notes and troubleshooting review
